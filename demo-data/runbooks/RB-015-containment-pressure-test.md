# RB-015: Containment Pressure Test

**Procedure:** RB-015  
**Revision:** 2.1  
**Date:** 2025-10-20  
**Classification:** Safety Test - Quarterly

## 1. Objective
Verify containment building integrity through pressure decay test per regulatory requirements.

## 2. Prerequisites
- Unit in cold shutdown (>48 hours)
- All containment penetrations closed and verified
- Shift Manager and Safety Officer approval
- Weather conditions: wind < 25 mph, no precipitation

## 3. Required Equipment
- Pressure test rig (Ref: PTR-CONT-01)
- Calibrated pressure gauges 0-20 psig (±0.1%)
- Leak detection equipment (ultrasonic detector)
- Data logger with 0.01 psi resolution

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Pre-Test Verification
1. Walk down all containment penetrations
2. Verify all airlocks closed and sealed
3. Check equipment hatch secured
4. Confirm personnel accountability (all personnel out)

### 4.2 Pressurization
1. Connect pressure test rig to test port TP-CONT-01
2. Pressurize at rate of 0.5 psi/min
3. Target pressure: 15.0 psig (±0.2 psi)
4. Monitor for rapid pressure drop (indicates major leak)

### 4.3 Stabilization
1. Hold at 15.0 psig for 30 minutes
2. Allow temperature stabilization
3. Record baseline pressure and temperature

### 4.4 Decay Test
1. Isolate test rig (close valve V-TEST-01)
2. Monitor pressure for 4 hours
3. Record pressure every 15 minutes
4. Acceptable decay: < 0.5 psi over 4 hours

### 4.5 Depressurization
1. Slowly vent through valve V-VENT-01
2. Depressurize at rate < 1.0 psi/min
3. Verify return to atmospheric pressure

## 5. Acceptance Criteria
- Pressure decay < 0.5 psi in 4 hours
- No audible leaks detected
- Temperature-corrected leak rate < 0.1% volume/day

## 6. Estimated Duration
**6 hours** (including stabilization)

## 7. References
- 10 CFR 50 Appendix J (Containment Leak Testing)
- ASME Section XI (Pressure Testing)
- Plant Technical Specifications 3.6.1
